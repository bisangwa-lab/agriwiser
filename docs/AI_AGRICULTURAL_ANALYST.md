# Innovation Three: Evidence-Grounded AI Agricultural Analyst

## 1. Core Mission & Anti-Hallucination Design

The AI Agricultural Analyst (`src/lib/intelligence/ai-analyst.js`) functions as an automated research partner for agricultural planners and statisticians.

Unlike generic conversational LLMs that hallucinate statistics, the AgriWiser AI Analyst is:
1. **Tool-Grounded**: All answers are generated by querying genuine NISR SAS datasets and climate reanalysis grids via verified tools.
2. **Strictly Classified**: Every claim is tagged as `OBSERVED_FACT`, `DERIVED_STATISTIC`, `SIMULATION_RESULT`, or `RECOMMENDATION`.
3. **Citation-Backed**: Every quantitative statement includes the exact publication, table, cell, or source URL.
4. **Deterministic Fallback**: Runs locally and reliably even when external LLM endpoints are offline.

---

## 2. Research Tools Catalog

The AI Analyst engine executes 10 specialized intelligence tools:

1. `searchDatasets`: Searches authorized NISR and climate datasets with metadata filters.
2. `getIndicatorDefinition`: Returns official statistical definitions and calculation formulas.
3. `queryAgriculturalObservations`: Queries granular district-level production, area, and yield figures.
4. `calculateStatistics`: Computes sample mean, median, min, max, variance, and standard deviation.
5. `compareDistricts`: Ranks and compares districts across a specific crop and season.
6. `compareAgriculturalPeriods`: Performs adjacent-season temporal comparisons.
7. `inspectDataQuality`: Audits missing value rates, observed zeros, and duplicate entries.
8. `getRiskAssessments`: Retrieves multi-signal risk evaluations from Innovation One.
9. `runScenario`: Invokes the Digital Twin simulator from Innovation Two.
10. `createReportDraft`: Drafts audit-ready policy briefs with embedded citations for human review.

---

## 3. Mandatory 6-Part Response Protocol

Every response produced by the AI Analyst strictly adheres to this structure:

1. **Direct Finding**: High-level factual answer based on data.
2. **Supporting Evidence**: Tabular list of official records with exact NISR source citations.
3. **Mathematical Calculation**: Explicit formula, showing baseline, current value, and zero-baseline protections.
4. **Interpretation**: Agronomic contextualization, explicitly separating correlation from causation.
5. **Uncertainty & Limitations**: Explicit disclosure of survey sampling error and missing data gaps.
6. **Recommended Action**: Suggested next steps for field verification or policy intervention.

---

## 4. UI Interaction

The AI Analyst is accessible via the **AI Analyst** tab in `AnalyticsDashboard.jsx`. Users can:
- Select from one-click preset research queries (e.g., "Season A Maize Declines", "Eastern Province Drought Review").
- Submit custom natural-language inquiries.
- Inspect structured findings, citations, and formulas.
- Export findings into draft Field Investigations.
